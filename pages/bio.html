About Me

I am a Bioinformatician at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute in the Center for Cancer Genomics. Previously, I earned a Ph.D. in Biomedical Informatics in the Department of Biomedical Informatics of Harvard Medical School. As a member of the Stephen Elledge Lab, I used high-throughput assays to study both the antibody-mediated immune response and the ubiquitin-proteasome system with amino-acid resolution.
